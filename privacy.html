Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Flagside is a flag football playbook app made by Brawley Developments ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We do not collect, see, sell, or share your information.
  • Everything you enter is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account.
  • Flagside has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • If you allow it, your approximate location is used only to show the field weather, and it's never saved or sent to us.

What the app stores

Flagside stores what you type or import into it: team details such as the name, colors, field size, and league rulebooks; your roster, including player names, jersey numbers, positions, wristband colors, and availability; and your plays, formations, lineups, practice plans, notes, games, and season archives.

Flagside does not ask for your phone number, date of birth, address, contacts, photos, or camera. It asks for your location only to show the weather at the field, as described below.

Rulebook imports

If you choose to import a league rulebook from a web link, Flagside connects directly to the web address you entered to download that page or PDF. If you choose a scanned PDF, Flagside reads the text entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Imported rulebook text is stored only on your device and in your iCloud account.

Field weather

If you allow it, Flagside uses your approximate location (to about a kilometer) while you're using the app to show the weather at the field during games and practices. Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service only to look up that forecast. It is not saved by Flagside, not stored in iCloud, and never sent to us.

You can turn this off any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, Flagside. Everything else in the app works without it.

Nearby devices

When you run a game or practice, Flagside can connect directly to other iPhones and iPads nearby that have the same team, such as your iPad or an assistant coach's phone, so the clock and game log stay in step even without an internet connection. The connection is encrypted, devices only exchange teams they both already have, and nothing passes through our servers.

You can turn this off any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, Flagside.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on your device (including a shared App Group container so your Home Screen widget and Lock Screen Live Activity can display the current game clock). If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it syncs across your devices. Apple holds this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access it.

Sharing with other coaches

A head coach can invite other coaches to a team. Invited coaches can see and edit that team's plays, roster, lineups, practices, and games. Sharing is handled by Apple through iCloud, and only the people you invite can see the team.

Players and children

Flagside is made for coaches and is not directed at children. Rosters often list young players, and that information is entered by the coach, not collected from the players. We recommend entering only what you need, such as a first name or initial and a jersey number. We never receive roster information.

Printing, sharing and support emails

When you print or share a play sheet, practice plan, or parent report, the PDF goes only where you choose to send it. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you.

AI and training

We do not use your information to train AI models, and we have no access to it to do so.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it.

  • Delete a team: open Team Settings and tap Delete Team. This removes the team's plays, roster, lineups, practices, and games for every coach on the team.
  • Delete all Flagside data from iCloud: open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Flagside, and Delete Data.
  • Delete data from a device: delete the app.
  • Get a copy of your plays: use Print Center to export your playbook as a PDF.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for significant changes, note them in the app's update notes.
