Flagside Support

Flagside is a playbook and game-day app for youth and rec flag football coaches. Design plays, manage your roster, plan practices, run the scoreboard and share your team with assistant coaches.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Flagside has no sign-up. Your teams are stored on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account so they sync across your devices.

How do I share a team with another coach?

Open your team's Team Settings and tap Invite or Manage Coaches. They'll get a link that opens the team in Flagside on their device. Invited coaches can see and edit plays, the roster, lineups and games. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

My team isn't syncing or sharing isn't working

  • Check that you're signed in to iCloud in the Settings app, and that iCloud Drive is on.
  • Make sure you have a network connection and some free iCloud storage.
  • Give it a minute: changes can take a short while to appear on other devices.
  • Still stuck? In Flagside, open About, run the Troubleshooting check, and email us the report.

How does the rules import work?

You can import your league's rules from a PDF (including scanned PDFs) or a web link, and Flagside picks out settings such as field size, players on the field and game length. On devices with Apple Intelligence, Flagside can read the rulebook more thoroughly. That reading happens entirely on your device; your rulebook is not sent to us or to any AI company.

Why can't I use the Apple Intelligence rules reader?

It needs an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. If it isn't available, Flagside tells you why, and the standard rules import still works.

How do I delete my data?

  • One team: in Flagside, open Team Settings and tap Delete Team.
  • Everything in iCloud: in the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Flagside, Delete Data.
  • From one device: delete the app.
